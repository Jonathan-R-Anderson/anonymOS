This is the signed statement of where the network lives. DNS is what breaks in every situation this exists for, so DNS is not the authority — this document is. Nodes hold the last directive they verified and prefer it over anything a name resolves to.

Nodes verify against a wallet address pinned in their own configuration, not one fetched from here. Being logged in as admin is not enough to issue a directive: the signature is the authority, because this travels to machines that have no session with us.
