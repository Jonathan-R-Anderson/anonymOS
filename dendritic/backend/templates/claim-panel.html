Claim your rewards

Rewards are not sent to you — they are claimed. When an epoch settles, the aggregator writes a Merkle root on-chain and your share becomes provable against it, but nothing moves until somebody makes the call. This page makes it.

3. Send the transaction

The reward is paid to the recipient address recorded in the settlement, not to whoever sends this. So anybody can submit it for you — if you have no ETH on Ethereum Mainnet for gas, somebody else can press this and the tokens still land in your wallet.

Why it takes three calls
  1. EpochManager.finalize(epoch) — closes the challenge window. Permissionless, so nobody can hold a settlement back.
  2. Treasury.fundEpoch(epoch) — mints exactly that epoch's rewards into the distributor. Also permissionless, and bounded by the epoch itself, so calling it gains the caller nothing except that people get paid.
  3. RewardDistributor.claim(…) — this page, with your proof.

Each is a separate transaction because each is separately checkable. A single button that did all three would also be a single button that could quietly skip one. See the epoch chain →
